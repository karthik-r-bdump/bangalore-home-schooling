# 📅 Curriculum Planning & Timetable

Successful homeschooling does not mean replicating a rigid 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM school timetable. Instead, it balances focused academic blocks with child-led inquiry, physical activities, and creative projects.

---

## 🗓️ Academic Year Schedule (ICSE Aligned)

The ICSE academic session in India typically runs from **April / June through March**:

| Term | Months | Focus Areas | Assessment Milestone |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Term 1 (Autumn)** | June – September | Core concepts, foundation building, science experiments | Formative Assessment 1 & Mid-Term Review |
| **Term 2 (Winter)** | October – December | Advanced topics, literature analysis, project submissions | Formative Assessment 2 & Project Evaluations |
| **Term 3 (Spring)** | January – March | Revision, past papers, problem solving, practicals | Summative Assessment / Mock Exams |

---

## ⏰ Sample Daily Timetables

### Primary (Classes 1 - 5)
- **08:30 – 09:30 AM**: Mathematics (Problem solving, hands-on manipulatives)
- **09:30 – 10:00 AM**: Break / Healthy Snack / Free Movement
- **10:00 – 11:00 AM**: English (Phonics, reading aloud, grammar & creative writing)
- **11:00 – 11:30 AM**: Second Language (Hindi / Kannada vocabulary, writing)
- **11:30 – 12:30 PM**: Environmental Studies / Science (Outdoor observation, experiments)
- **Afternoon**: Free reading, library visit, sports (swimming, cycling, martial arts), arts & crafts.

### Middle & Secondary (Classes 6 - 10)
- **08:30 – 10:00 AM**: Block 1: Mathematics or Physics (Deep focus, problem solving)
- **10:15 – 11:45 AM**: Block 2: Chemistry / Biology / Computer Applications
- **12:00 – 01:00 PM**: Block 3: History & Civics or Geography (Map work, case studies)
- **02:00 – 03:00 PM**: Block 4: English Language (Grammar, essays, comprehension) or Literature
- **03:00 – 04:00 PM**: Second Language / Self-Study / Coding project
- **Evening**: Physical education, sports club, music, or community engagement.
