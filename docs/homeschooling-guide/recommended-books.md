# 📚 Recommended Textbooks & Publishers for ICSE

While CISCE provides syllabi and regulations, individual ICSE schools choose from reputable private publishers. Below are the most widely recognized textbooks recommended for homeschoolers:

---

## 📖 Primary (Classes 1 - 5)
- **English**:
  - *New Oxford Modern English* (OUP) or *Gul Mohar* (Orient BlackSwan)
  - *Wren & Martin Elementary English Grammar*
- **Mathematics**:
  - *Foundation Mathematics* by R.S. Aggarwal (Goyal Brothers)
  - *Understanding Mathematics* by M.L. Aggarwal (Avichal Publishing Company - APC)
- **Science / EVS**:
  - *Frank Environmental Studies* (Macmillan / Frank Brothers)
  - *Living Science* (Ratna Sagar)
- **Social Studies**:
  - *Footprints* (Macmillan Education) or *My Wonderful Book of Social Studies* (Cordova)
- **Computer Studies**:
  - *Kips Computers* or *Logix 2.0* (Kips Learning)

---

## 🔬 Middle School (Classes 6 - 8)
- **Physics**: *Concise Physics* (Selina Publishers) or *Living Science Physics* (Ratna Sagar)
- **Chemistry**: *Concise Chemistry* (Selina Publishers) or *Simplified ICSE Chemistry* (Allied)
- **Biology**: *Concise Biology* (Selina Publishers)
- **Mathematics**: *Understanding ICSE Mathematics* by M.L. Aggarwal (APC) or *Concise Mathematics* by R.K. Bansal (Selina)
- **History & Civics**: *Past & Present* (Ratna Sagar) or *Total History & Civics* (Morning Star)
- **Geography**: *A Text Book of ICSE Geography* by Veena Bhargava (Goyal Brothers)
- **English**: *Total English* (Morning Star) and *Wren & Martin High School English Grammar*

---

## 🎓 Secondary (Classes 9 & 10 - ICSE Board)
- **English**:
  - *Total English Class 9 & 10* by Xavier Pinto & P. Pinto (Morning Star)
  - Prescribed Literature texts: Shakespeare's *Julius Caesar* (Morning Star Edition with paraphrase), *Treasure Chest: A Collection of ICSE Poems and Short Stories* (Evergreen)
- **Mathematics**:
  - *Understanding ICSE Mathematics Class 9 & 10* by M.L. Aggarwal (APC)
  - *Concise Mathematics Class 9 & 10* by R.K. Bansal (Selina)
- **Physics**: *Concise Physics Class 9 & 10* (Selina Publishers)
- **Chemistry**: *Simplified Middle School / ICSE Chemistry* by Dr. Viraf J. Dalal (Allied Publishers)
- **Biology**: *Concise Biology Class 9 & 10* by Dr. H.S. Vishnoi (Selina Publishers)
- **History & Civics**: *Total History & Civics Class 9 & 10* (Morning Star)
- **Geography**: *Total Geography Class 9 & 10* by Jasmine Rachel & Dolly E. Sequeira (Morning Star)
- **Computer Applications**: *Understanding Computer Applications with BlueJ* by Vijay Kumar Pandey & Dilip Kumar Dey (APC)
