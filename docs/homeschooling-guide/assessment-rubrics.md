# 📊 Assessment Rubrics & Portfolios

In a homeschool setting, assessment is formative—designed to guide and encourage the learner rather than produce anxiety.

---

## 📁 1. The Homeschool Portfolio

A learning portfolio is an authentic record of a student's intellectual growth over the year:
- **Work Samples**: Best creative writing pieces, lab experiment reports, math problem sheets.
- **Reading Log**: Titles, authors, and brief reflections for all fiction and non-fiction books read.
- **Projects & Presentations**: Photographs or links to models, coding projects, artwork, and science exhibits.
- **Periodic Self-Evaluations**: Student reflections on what was easy, what was challenging, and goals for the next term.

---

## 📝 2. ICSE-Aligned Question Rubrics

For Classes 6 through 10, align your practice tests with the CISCE marking standards:

| Question Type | Standard Marks | Expected Answer Characteristics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Objective / MCQ** | 1 Mark | Direct factual recall or single-step computation. |
| **Short Answer (Definition / Reason)** | 2 Marks | Precise scientific definition or statement with 2 key points / keywords. |
| **Short Structured** | 3 Marks | Clear 3-point explanation, derivation, or labeled diagram. |
| **Long Structured Question** | 4 – 5 Marks | Multi-part question testing comprehension, application, calculation, and diagrammatic representation. |
