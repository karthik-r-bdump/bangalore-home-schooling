# 🌐 Digital Assets & Learning Tools

Enhance your homeschooling journey with these curated interactive simulations, open-source repositories, and multimedia tools:

---

## 🔬 Interactive Science & Math Simulations
- **PhET Interactive Simulations (University of Colorado Boulder)**:
  - Free interactive simulations for Physics (Circuits, Force, Light), Chemistry (Atomic structure, Balancing equations, Solutions), and Math (Fractions, Graphing).
  - Web: [phet.colorado.edu](https://phet.colorado.edu)
- **GeoGebra**:
  - Dynamic mathematics software for geometry, algebra, 3D graphing, and calculus.
  - Web: [geogebra.org](https://www.geogebra.org)

---

## 💻 Computer Science & Coding
- **BlueJ IDE**: The official interactive Java environment used in ICSE Class 9 & 10 Computer Applications. [bluej.org](https://bluej.org)
- **Scratch (MIT)**: Block-based programming for primary and middle schoolers. [scratch.mit.edu](https://scratch.mit.edu)
- **Python.org**: Standard language for Middle School and secondary scripting.

---

## 🗺️ Geography & Map Practice
- **Bhuvan (ISRO)**: Indian Geo-platform of ISRO with satellite imagery and thematic maps of India.
- **Survey of India**: Standard 45D/7 and 45D/10 Topographical Sheets used for ICSE Class 10 Board exam practice.
