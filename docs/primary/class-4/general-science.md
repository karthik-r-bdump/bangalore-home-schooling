# Class 4 General Science
## Topics Covered
- Living & Non-Living Things, Plant Anatomy & Photosynthesis, Animal Classification.
- Human Body Systems (Digestive, Respiratory, Circulatory, Skeletal).
- Matter (Solids, Liquids, Gases), Force, Work, and Energy.
- Earth, Moon, Solar System, and Environmental Conservation.
