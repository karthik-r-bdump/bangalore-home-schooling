# 🎒 Primary Stage Overview (Classes 1 to 5)

The ICSE primary curriculum emphasizes inquiry-based learning, strong mathematical computation, expressive language proficiency, and experiential environmental understanding.

---

## 📚 Subject Framework
1. **English**: Literature reader, language grammar, creative composition, comprehension passages.
2. **Mathematics**: Arithmetic operations, word problems, geometry, money, time, measurement, and data handling.
3. **Environmental Studies (Classes 1-2)** / **General Science (Classes 3-5)**: Plants, animals, human body systems, forces, materials, universe.
4. **Social Studies (Classes 3-5)**: Our community, history of early civilizations, Indian geography, climate, and citizenship.
5. **Second Language (Hindi / Regional)**: Grammar, reading fluency, vocabulary, and composition.
6. **Computer Studies**: Digital literacy, keyboarding, MS Paint/Office, logic, and early block programming.
