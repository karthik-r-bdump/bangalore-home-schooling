# Class 1 Mathematics
## Syllabus & Topics
- Number concepts, place value up to 100, operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division).
- Fractions, decimals (Classes 4-5), measurement (length, weight, capacity), money, time, geometry (2D/3D shapes, perimeter, area).
- Word problems and mental math strategies.

## 🧮 Sample Notes & Formulas
- Division Algorithm: $\text{Dividend} = (\text{Divisor} \times \text{Quotient}) + \text{Remainder}$
- Perimeter of Rectangle: $P = 2 \times (\text{length} + \text{breadth})$

## 📝 Practice Questions
1. Solve: $456 \times 12$ and verify the result.
2. A rectangular garden has length $15\text{ m}$ and breadth $8\text{ m}$. Find its perimeter and area.
