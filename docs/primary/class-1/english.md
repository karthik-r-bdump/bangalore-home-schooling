# Class 1 English
## Syllabus & Core Topics
- **Grammar**: Parts of speech, sentence construction, punctuation, tenses, prepositions, articles.
- **Vocabulary**: Synonyms, antonyms, prefixes/suffixes, idioms suitable for Class 1.
- **Writing Skills**: Paragraph writing, informal letters, picture composition, story completion.
- **Reading Comprehension**: Unseen passages with factual and inferential questions.

## 📝 Sample Practice Questions
1. **Grammar**: Identify the noun and verb in: *"The energetic puppy chased the red ball across the garden."*
2. **Composition Prompt**: Write a short paragraph (60-80 words) describing *"My Favorite Rainy Day in Bangalore"*.
