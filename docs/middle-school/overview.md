# 🔬 Middle School Stage Overview (Classes 6 to 8)

The Middle School years are pivotal in the ICSE system. General Science diverges into **Physics, Chemistry, and Biology**, while Social Studies branches into **History & Civics** and **Geography**.

---

## 🎯 Academic Goals
- **Analytical Thinking**: Applying formulas, balancing chemical equations, solving algebraic equations.
- **Scientific Method**: Designing simple home experiments, observing outcomes, drawing labeled diagrams.
- **ICSE Foundation**: Laying conceptual groundwork directly tested in Class 9 & 10 Board examinations.
