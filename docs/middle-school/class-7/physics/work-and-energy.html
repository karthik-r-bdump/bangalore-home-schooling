 WORK DONE: W = Force x displacement in the direction of force = F x s
 Definition of 1 joule: One joule is the work done when a force of 1 N displaces a body through 1 m in the direction of the force. Work is a scalar
 quantity.


 2. What is energy?
                                           Energy is the capacity to do work. Work and energy
                                           have the same SI unit: joule (J).
                                           Definition of 1 calorie: One calorie is the heat required to
                                           raise the temperature of 1 g of water by 1 degree C (from
                                           14.5 degree C to 15.5 degree C).
                                           1 cal is approximately 4.2 J.


 Forms: Mechanical | Heat | Light | Sound | Electrical | Chemical | Solar | Nuclear


 3. Key energy definitions
 Mechanical energy: Energy possessed by a body due to its motion,              Electrical energy: Energy associated with moving electric charges or
 position or configuration. It is the sum of kinetic and potential energies.   supplied by an electric current.

 Kinetic energy: Energy possessed by a body by virtue of its motion.           Potential energy: Stored energy possessed by a body by virtue of its
                                                                               position or configuration.


 4. Mechanical energy formulas
  KINETIC ENERGY (KE)                                                           POTENTIAL ENERGY (PE)
