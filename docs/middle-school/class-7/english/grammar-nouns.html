📖 Nouns & Types of Nouns

📝 Study Notes & Solved Exercises​

Chapter 01: Nouns



Section A Task: Underline the nouns in the following passage and write (P) for proper nouns and (C) for
common nouns.

"Many kings ruled ancient India over the ages. Among the greatest of them was Emperor Ashoka. He was
known as a fierce and brave warrior. But the war of Kalinga brought a complete change in him. He was deeply
disturbed by the many lives lost in the war. From that time, he gave up violence and became a follower of the
Buddha. He sent his children his daughter Sanghamitra and son Mahindra to spread Buddhism to Sri Lanka, a
far-off foreign land. The spread of Buddhism in Asian countries such as China, Japan, Cambodia and Laos is
because of the efforts of Ashoka."



Section B Task: Read the sentences and write whether the nouns given in italics are countable (C) or
uncountable (U).

    1.   How much cheese do you need?

    2.   Meenu has friends all over the neighbourhood.

    3.   I like strawberry milkshake.

    4.   They needed gallons of water to put out the fire.

    5.   Abbas always has orange juice in the morning.

    6.   Dogs are faithful animals.

    7.   Neelam eats brown rice three times a week.

    8.   Delete all the old messages on your mobile phone.

Section C (Sequential section under Collective Nouns) Task: Fill in the blanks with the correct options (using
words like gaggle, horde, bundle, crowd, swarm, bunch, bouquet, or gang).

    1.   a ___ of thieves

    2.   a ___ of geese

    3.   a ___ of locusts

    4.   a ___ of flowers

    5.   a ___ of grapes

    6.   a ___ of clothes

    7.   a ___ of people

    8.   a ___ of barbarians

Section D (Sequential section after Collective Nouns) Task: Identify the nouns in the following sentences and
write if they are proper (P), common (C), collective (CO) or abstract (A).

    1.   Hard work never goes to waste.

    2.   There is no shortcut to success.
    3.   The police have caught the criminal.

    4.   The committee has prepared the petition.

    5.   Emperor Vikramaditya was a great patron of the arts.

    6.   The cattle are grazing in the field.

    7.   She sent me a bouquet of flowers.

    8.   Hyderabad is the capital of the newly-formed state of Telangana.

    9.   Faith can create miracles.

Section E Task: Underline the material nouns in these sentences.

    1.   Gold is a good conductor of heat.

    2.   Jute is one of the cheapest natural fibres.

    3.   The child is sailing a boat made of paper.

    4.   Plastic is not good for the environment.

    5.   Rubber is a bad conductor of electricity.

    6.   Some people think that food tastes better when cooked in utensils made of clay.

Section F Task: Write the plural forms of the following compound nouns.

    1.   passer-by

    2.   ladybird

    3.   water tank

    4.   printer cartridge

    5.   washing machine

    6.   daughter-in-law

Section G (Sequential section after F) Task: Choose the correct nouns to complete the sentences.

    1.   They are buying (furniture/furnitures) for their new house.

    2.   This railway track is used for (goods/good) trains.

    3.   I have no further (information/informations) regarding her arrival.

    4.   He is older than his brother by two and a half (year/years).

    5.   She lost her bunch of (key/keys) in the train yesterday.

    6.   The accident was seen by many (passer-bys/passers-by).

    7.   He has seven (son-in-laws/sons-in-law).

    8.   The two former (chiefs of staff/chief of staffs) are attending the meeting.
