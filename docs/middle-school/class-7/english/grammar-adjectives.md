# 📖 Adjectives & Degrees of Comparison

## 📝 Study Notes & Solved Exercises

```markdown
02 Adjectives

   •   Underline the adjectives in the passage: "The grey elephant was walking behind a white taxi.
       Suddenly, it raised its large trunk and let out a loud trumpet. A little girl peeked out from the old taxi.
       As soon as the elephant saw her, he brought his trunk down and waited. The girl stepped out of the
       taxi. She had two bananas in her small hands. The elephant took the yellow bananas, ate them and
       walked away on the dusty road".

Section A

   •   Underline the adjectives in the following sentences and mention their types:

            1.   My niece is a jovial person; everyone likes her.

            2.   We have five pens; we need to buy three more.

            3.   Indian films are loved all over the world.

            4.   Many people know that she is the daughter of a famous film star.

            5.   Which shirt do you like more, this one or that one?

            6.   The audience was spellbound by the tricks of the skilful magician.

            7.   I have photocopied those documents.

            8.   If you go to the market, buy some sugar and a dozen eggs.

            9.   Our luggage is packed; we are ready.

            10. I had a busy day in office today; there were several meetings.

Section B

   •   Fill in the blanks with the appropriate adjectives:

            1.   Sit in your _______ chair. (actual/own/very)

            2.   The _______ ceremony is a beautiful cultural activity. (Japan/Japanese)

            3.   There are _______ options available. (none/own/six)

            4.   What a _______ flower! (some/all/pretty)

            5.   _______ of the women were upset. (Any/Some/One)

            6.   She looks _______. (Europe/European/Europe-like)

            7.   I bought _______ mangoes from the seller. (actual/nine/sixth)

            8.   The _______ house from the left is Mr Mukherjee's. (fourth/own/four)

Formation of Adjectives

   •   Fill in the blanks with adjectives formed from the given words:

            1.   The teacher often scolds Reba because she is talkative. (talk)

            2.   My uncle was a book worm (book) child. He would always be found in the school library.
           3.   Pam always looked Cheerful (cheer). No one knew that she had a Tragedis (tragedy) history.

           4.   Though his relatives denied it, Shamik was the _______ (law) owner of the property.

           5.   His _______ (haste) decision cost him the respect of his _______ (love) ones.

Compound Adjectives

   •   Fill in the blanks with the given compound adjectives: (state-of-the-art, mouth-watering, weak-
       kneed, man-eating, long-lasting, strong-willed)

           1.   We have _______ laboratories in our school.

           2.   Martin Luther King Jr's speech had a _______ impact on humanity.

           3.   My aunt is a _______ woman; she is not easily influenced by external pressure.

           4.   Among the many attractions of old Delhi is its _______ street food.

           5.   The _______ tiger entered the village silently.

           6.   The _______ lord does not have the courage to speak up for the interests of his men in front
                of the king.

Order of Adjectives

   •   Choose the option that has the correct order of adjectives:

           1.   I am going to wear my _______ shirt for my cousin's wedding. (a. new, silk, purple b. new,
                purple, silk c. silk, purple, new d. purple, new, silk)

           2.   We need to recycle the _______ cans. (a. six, plastic, round b. round, plastic, six c. six, round,
                plastic d. plastic, six, round)

           3.   Raman is a _______ man. (a. tall, generous, thin b. thin, tall, generous c. generous, tall, thin d.
                generous, thin, tall)

           4.   I am going to drink tea from a _______ cup. (a. small, English, china b. English, small, china c.
                small, china, English d. china, small, English)

           5.   The _______ teacher spoke for hours. (a. interesting, German, old b. interesting, old, German
                c. old, interesting, German d. German, interesting, old)
```
