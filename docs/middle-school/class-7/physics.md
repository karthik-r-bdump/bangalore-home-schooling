# Class 7 Physics
## Syllabus & Core Topics
- Physical Quantities and Measurement (Volume, Density, Speed).
- Force and Motion (Types of motion, Newton's laws introduction, Friction).
- Energy, Work, and Power.
- Light (Reflection, Spherical mirrors, Refraction basics).
- Heat & Temperature, Sound waves, Magnetism and Electricity.

## ⚡ Key Principles
- Density formula: $\rho = \frac{m}{V}$ (Units: $\text{kg/m}^3$ or $\text{g/cm}^3$).
- Work done: $W = F \times d$.

## 📝 Practice Questions
1. A solid brass cylinder of mass $850\text{ g}$ has a volume of $100\text{ cm}^3$. Find its density in SI units.
2. Explain the difference between mass and weight with four distinct points.
