## 📋 Common Valencies of Radicals

| Radical | Formula | Valency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Hydroxide | $\text{OH}^-$ | 1 |
| Nitrate | $\text{NO}_3^-$ | 1 |
| Bicarbonate | $\text{HCO}_3^-$ | 1 |
| Carbonate | $\text{CO}_3^{2-}$ | 2 |
| Sulphate | $\text{SO}_4^{2-}$ | 2 |
