# Class 7 Biology
## Syllabus & Topics
- Plant Life: Leaf structure, Transpiration, Photosynthesis, Flower structure, Pollination.
- Cell: Structure and function of plant and animal cells.
- Human Body: Digestive, Respiratory, Circulatory, Nervous, and Excretory systems.
- Health, Hygiene, Diseases, and First Aid.
- Ecosystems and Conservation.

## 🔬 Key Diagrams to Master
- Structure of a generalized Plant and Animal Cell.
- Parts of a typical flower (Calyx, Corolla, Androecium, Gynoecium).
