⚔️ The Vijayanagar and Bahmani Kingdoms

During the reign of Mohammad-bin-Tughlaq, two powerful regional kingdoms arose in South India and the Deccan: the Vijayanagar Empire and the Bahmani Kingdom.

🏰 1. The Vijayanagar Empire (1336 – 1646 CE)​

  • Founders: Harihara I and Bukka Raya I of the Sangama dynasty in 1336 CE, on the banks of river Tungabhadra (Hampi, Karnataka).
  • Greatest Ruler: Krishnadeva Raya (1509 – 1529 CE) of the Tuluva dynasty:
    • Military victories over the Sultan of Bijapur, Gajapatis of Odisha, and ruler of Golconda.
    • Patron of arts, literature, and architecture. Authored Amuktamalyada (Telugu poem on statecraft).
    • Maintained cordial relations with the Portuguese (governor Albuquerque).
    • Famous court poet and advisor: Tenali Ramakrishna.
  • Decline: Battle of Talikota (1565 CE) where the combined armies of Deccan Sultanates defeated Vijayanagar.

🕌 2. The Bahmani Kingdom (1347 – 1527 CE)​

  • Founder: Ala-ud-din Bahman Shah (Hasan Gangu) in 1347 CE. Capital at Gulbarga (Kalaburagi), later shifted to Bidar.
  • Mahmud Gawan: Capable Prime Minister (Wazir) who expanded territory, reformed administration, and built the famous Madrasa at Bidar.
  • Breakup: Disintegrated into five Deccan Sultanates: Bijapur, Golconda, Ahmadnagar, Bidar, and Berar.

 SECTION A: BRIEF ANSWER QUESTIONS
 1. "The disintegration of the Delhi Sultanate provided an opportunity for the rise of number of kingdoms
 in the South." Mention any two.
 Verbatim Source Answer: "In the Deccan, after the decline of the Tughlaqs, there arose two important
 kingdoms, namely, the Vijayanagar and the Bahmani."

 2. State the extent of Vijayanagar Empire during Harihar time period.
 Verbatim Source Answer: "The Vijayanagar empire then comprised the whole of Southern India below the
 river Tungabhadra, covering the present territories of Chennai and Mysore up to Cape Comorin."

 3. Name the greatest ruler of the Vijayanagar kingdom.
 Verbatim Source Answer: "Krishna Deva Raya was the greatest ruler of Vijayanagar."

 4. Who was Hasan Gangu ?
 Verbatim Source Answer: "The sultanate was founded in 1347 by the Turkish Governor of Muhammad bin
 Tughlaq, Hasan Gangu, who later on took the title Ala-ud-din Hassan Bahman Shah."

 5. Name the last ruler of the Vijayanagar Kingdom.
 Verbatim Source Answer: "The last ruler of this dynasty was Sada Shiva, who was defeated by the Bahmani
 State in the Battle of Talikot in 1565 AD."

 6. Name any two travellers who visited Vijayanagar in 1420 and 1443 AD respectively.
 Verbatim Source Answer: "Italian traveller Nicolo-Conti and Persian traveller, Abdul Razzak, who visited
 Vijayanagar in 1420 and 1443 AD respectively, had praised the kingdom for its all-round progress and
 prosperity."

 7. What was a Kattam?
 Verbatim Source Answer: "Kattam: District under Vijayanagar kingdom."
