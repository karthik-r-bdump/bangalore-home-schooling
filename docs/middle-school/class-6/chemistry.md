# Class 6 Chemistry
## Syllabus & Topics
- Introduction to Chemistry, Laboratory apparatus and safety.
- Elements, Compounds, and Mixtures (Separation techniques).
- Matter and its composition (Kinetic theory of matter).
- Atomic Structure, Valency, and Chemical Formulae.
- Language of Chemistry: Writing chemical equations and balancing them.
- Air, Water, Acids, Bases, and Salts.

## 🧪 Chemical Equations
$$2\text{H}_2 + \text{O}_2 \rightarrow 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$$
$$\text{CaCO}_3 \xrightarrow{\Delta} \text{CaO} + \text{CO}_2\uparrow$$
