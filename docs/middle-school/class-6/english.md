# Class 6 English
## Language
- Functional Grammar: Tenses, active/passive voice, direct/indirect speech, prepositions, synthesis of sentences.
- Composition: Narrative, descriptive, imaginative essays (250-300 words).
- Letter Writing: Formal (editor, municipal commissioner) and Informal (friends, family).
- Notice and Email Writing (CISCE format).
## Literature
- Detailed study of prescribed prose and poetry selections.
