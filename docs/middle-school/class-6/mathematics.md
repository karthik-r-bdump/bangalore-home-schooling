# Class 6 Mathematics
## Syllabus & Major Chapters
- **Number Systems**: Integers, Rational Numbers, Exponents, Squares and Square Roots, Cubes.
- **Algebra**: Algebraic Expressions, Linear Equations in One Variable, Factorisation.
- **Commercial Mathematics**: Ratio & Proportion, Unitary Method, Percentage, Profit & Loss, Simple Interest.
- **Geometry & Mensuration**: Angles, Triangles, Quadrilaterals, Circles, Perimeter, Area, Surface Area & Volume.
- **Statistics**: Data representation, Mean, Median, Mode, Bar graphs.

## 📐 Key Formulas & Notes
- Simple Interest: $I = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$
- Area of Triangle: $A = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$

## 📝 Practice Questions
1. Solve for $x$: $3(2x - 5) + 4 = 2(x + 3) - 7$.
2. Calculate the simple interest on ₹12,000 invested at $7.5\%$ per annum for 3 years.
