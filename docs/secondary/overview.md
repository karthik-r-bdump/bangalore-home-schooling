# 🎓 Secondary Stage (Classes 9 & 10 - ICSE Board)

The ICSE (Class 10) Examination conducted by the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE) is one of the most prestigious secondary credentials in India and globally.

---

## 🏛️ ICSE Subject Groups

| Group | Subjects | Examination Weightage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Group I (Compulsory)** | 1. English (Paper 1 & Paper 2)<br/>2. Second Language (Hindi / Regional)<br/>3. History, Civics & Geography (Paper 1 & Paper 2) | External Exam: $80\%$<br/>Internal Assessment: $20\%$ |
| **Group II (Choose 2 or 3)** | 1. Mathematics<br/>2. Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)<br/>3. Commercial Studies / Economics | External Exam: $80\%$<br/>Internal Assessment: $20\%$ |
| **Group III (Choose 1)** | 1. Computer Applications<br/>2. Commercial Applications<br/>3. Art / Physical Education / Home Science | External Exam: $50\%$ / $100\%$<br/>Internal Assessment: $50\%$ / $100\%$ |

---

## 🎯 Homeschooling Strategy for ICSE 10th
1. **Specimen Papers**: CISCE releases official specimen question papers annually reflecting exact question patterns and mark distributions.
2. **Step-by-Step Mark Allocation**: In Math and Sciences, marks are allocated for formulas, substitution, unit conversion, and final answer with correct units.
3. **Internal Assessment Projects**: Completing the 20-mark internal projects (English listening/speaking, science lab experiments, history/geography projects, computer programs).
