# Class 9 Chemistry (Science Paper 2 - 80 Marks)

## Syllabus Chapters
1. The Language of Chemistry\n2. Chemical Changes & Reactions\n3. Water\n4. Atomic Structure & Chemical Bonding\n5. The Periodic Table\n6. Study of Hydrogen\n7. Gas Laws (Boyle's and Charles' Law)

## 🧪 Crucial Reactions & Equations
- Boyle's Law: $P_1 V_1 = P_2 V_2$ at constant temperature.\n- Charles' Law: $\frac{V_1}{T_1} = \frac{V_2}{T_2}$ at constant pressure.
