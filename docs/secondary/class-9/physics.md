# Class 9 Physics (Science Paper 1 - 80 Marks)

## Syllabus Chapters
1. Measurements & Experimentation (Vernier calipers, screw gauge, simple pendulum)\n2. Motion in One Dimension\n3. Laws of Motion\n4. Fluids & Archimedes' Principle\n5. Heat & Energy\n6. Light (Reflection, Spherical mirrors)\n7. Sound\n8. Electricity & Magnetism

## ⚡ Key Laws & Equations
- Time Period of Simple Pendulum: $T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{l}{g}}$\n- Relative Density: $\text{RD} = \frac{\text{Weight of body in air}}{\text{Loss of weight in water}}$

## 📝 Practice Questions with Marking Scheme
1. Derive the relationship between Newton and dyne.
2. State Archimedes' Principle. Explain why an iron nail sinks in water while an iron ship floats.
