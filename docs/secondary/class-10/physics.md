# Class 10 Physics (Science Paper 1 - 80 Marks)

## Syllabus Chapters
1. Force, Work, Power and Energy\n2. Light (Refraction, Total Internal Reflection, Lenses, Spectrum)\n3. Sound (Echoes, Vibrations)\n4. Electricity (Ohm's law, EMF, Internal resistance, Electrical power)\n5. Electromagnetism\n6. Calorimetry (Principle of Mixtures)\n7. Radioactivity (Alpha, Beta, Gamma emissions, Nuclear energy)

## ⚡ Key Laws & Equations
- Snell's Law: $\mu = \frac{\sin i}{\sin r} = \frac{c}{v}$\n- Electrical Power: $P = VI = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R}$\n- Heat Exchange: $Q = mc\Delta T$

## 📝 Practice Questions with Marking Scheme
1. A body of mass $200\text{ g}$ falls freely from a height of $10\text{ m}$. Calculate its kinetic energy when it is halfway down ($g = 9.8\text{ m/s}^2$).
2. Draw a ray diagram to show the formation of a virtual, erect, and magnified image by a convex lens. State the position of the object.
