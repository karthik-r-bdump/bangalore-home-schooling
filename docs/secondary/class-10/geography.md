# Class 10 Geography (Paper 2 - 80 Marks)

## Section A: Map & Toposheet Work (30 Marks)
- Part I: Interpretation of Topographical Maps (20 Marks) - 4-figure & 6-figure grid references, contours, drainage patterns, settlements, occupation identification.\n- Part II: Outline Map of India (10 Marks) - Mountains, rivers, cities, plateaus, winds, minerals.

## Section B: Geography of India (50 Marks)
1. Climate of India (Monsoon mechanism)\n2. Soils of India (Alluvial, Black, Red, Laterite)\n3. Natural Vegetation (Tropical Evergreen, Deciduous, Thorn, Tidal, Mountain)\n4. Water Resources (Irrigation methods, Rainwater harvesting)\n5. Mineral & Energy Resources (Iron, Coal, Petroleum, Solar, Wind)\n6. Agriculture (Rice, Wheat, Millets, Cotton, Jute, Tea, Coffee)\n7. Manufacturing Industries (Iron & Steel, Cotton, Petrochemicals)\n8. Transport (Roadways, Railways, Airways, Waterways)\n9. Waste Management
