# Class 10 Economics & Commercial Studies

## Topics Covered
- Factors of Production: Land, Labour, Capital, Enterprise and their characteristics.\n- Theory of Demand and Supply: Law of Demand, Elasticity of Demand, Law of Supply.\n- Money and Banking: Functions of Money, Commercial Banks, Central Bank (RBI) monetary policy.\n- Inflation: Types, causes, and control measures.\n- Consumer Awareness & Rights (Consumer Protection Act).
