# Class 10 English Language (Paper 1 - 80 Marks)

## Examination Pattern
- **Question 1 (20 Marks)**: Composition (300-350 words) - Narrative, Descriptive, Argumentative, Story writing, or Picture composition.
- **Question 2 (10 Marks)**: Letter Writing (Formal or Informal).
- **Question 3 (10 Marks)**: Notice Writing (5 Marks) and Email Writing (5 Marks) in strict CISCE format.
- **Question 4 (20 Marks)**: Unseen Prose Comprehension (Word meanings, short questions, and 50-word précis/summary).
- **Question 5 (20 Marks)**: Functional Grammar (Tenses blank-filling, prepositions, sentence synthesis without 'and/but/so', sentence transformation).

## 📝 High-Yield Grammar Tips
- **Transformation of Sentences**:
  - No sooner... than: *"No sooner did the bell ring than the students left."*
  - Hardly... when: *"Hardly had the bell rung when the students left."*
  - Degrees of Comparison, Active/Passive Voice, Direct/Indirect Speech.
