# Class 10 Literature in English (Paper 2 - 80 Marks)

## Prescribed Texts
1. **Drama**: Shakespeare's *Julius Caesar*
   - Act-wise scene summary, character motivations, rhetorical devices (Brutus vs. Antony funeral orations).
2. **Poetry**: *Treasure Chest: A Collection of ICSE Poems*
   - Themes, stanza-by-stanza analysis, poetic devices (metaphor, personification, alliteration, symbolism).
3. **Prose / Short Stories**: *Treasure Chest: A Collection of ICSE Short Stories*
   - Plot summary, character sketches, themes, extract-based questions.

## ✍️ Extract-Based Question Strategy
- Quote accuracy and context identification (Who said this to whom? What led up to this moment?).
- Explain figurative meaning rather than merely restating literal lines.
