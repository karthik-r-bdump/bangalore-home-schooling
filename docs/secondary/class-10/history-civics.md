# Class 10 History & Civics (Paper 1 - 80 Marks)

## Section A: Civics
- The Union Legislature: Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha (powers, functions, procedures)\n- The Union Executive: President, Vice-President, Prime Minister and Council of Ministers\n- The Judiciary: Supreme Court, High Courts, Subordinate Courts & Lok Adalats

## Section B: History
- The Indian National Movement (1857 - 1917): Revolt of 1857, Early Nationalists, Assertive Nationalists, Partition of Bengal\n- Mass Phase of the National Movement (1915 - 1947): Mahatma Gandhi, Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience, Quit India, Subhash Chandra Bose & INA, Independence & Partition\n- Contemporary World: First World War, Rise of Dictatorships (Fascism & Nazism), Second World War, United Nations, Non-Aligned Movement (NAM)
