# General Awareness (Pre-KG)
- **Myself**: Name, age, favorite food, body parts (head, eyes, ears, nose, hands, legs).
- **My Family**: Father, Mother, Siblings, Grandparents.
- **Seasons & Nature**: Sunny days, rainy days (using umbrellas, paper boats), day and night.
