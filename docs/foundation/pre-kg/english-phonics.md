# English & Phonics (Pre-KG)
## Syllabus & Objectives
- Sound discrimination: Listening to animal sounds, vehicle sounds, household noises.
- Introduction to Jolly Phonics Group 1 sounds: `/s/`, `/a/`, `/t/`, `/i/`, `/p/`, `/n/`.
- Action rhymes and picture reading.
## Weekly Activities
1. **Sound Hunt**: Find items in the room starting with `/s/` (spoon, sock, soap).
2. **Sandpaper Letters**: Trace letter shapes with fingers while vocalizing the sound.
