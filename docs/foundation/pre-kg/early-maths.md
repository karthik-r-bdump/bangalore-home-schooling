# Early Numeracy & Pre-Math (Pre-KG)
## Concepts Covered
- **Attributes**: Big vs. Small, Tall vs. Short, Heavy vs. Light.
- **Colors**: Red, Blue, Yellow, Green.
- **Shapes**: Circle, Square, Triangle.
- **Counting**: Rote counting numbers 1 to 10 with physical objects (counting blocks, leaves, shells).
