# English & Phonics (LKG)
- Recognition and writing of uppercase letters A to Z and lowercase a to z.
- Phonic sounds for all 26 letters.
- Identification of beginning sounds in words (e.g., `B` for Ball, `C` for Cat).
- Basic sight words: `I`, `a`, `the`, `is`, `on`.
