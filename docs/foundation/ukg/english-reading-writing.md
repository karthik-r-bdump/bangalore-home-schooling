# English Reading & Writing (UKG)
- **CVC Words (Consonant-Vowel-Consonant)**:
  - Short 'a': *cat, bat, mat, fan, cap*
  - Short 'e': *bed, red, hen, pen, leg*
  - Short 'i': *pin, tin, bin, lip, sit*
  - Short 'o': *dog, log, pot, cot, box*
  - Short 'u': *sun, bun, cup, tub, nut*
- Reading simple decodable sentences: *"The cat is on the mat."*
- Punctuation basics: Capital letters to start, full stops to end.
