# 🌱 Foundation Stage Overview (Pre-KG to UKG)

The Foundation Stage covers early childhood development from ages 3 to 6:
- **Pre-KG (Nursery)**: Age 3+
- **LKG (Junior KG / PP1)**: Age 4+
- **UKG (Senior KG / PP2)**: Age 5+

---

## 🎯 CISCE Early Years Objectives
1. **Physical & Motor Development**: Gross motor (jumping, balancing, running) and fine motor (finger grasp, scissor cutting, threading, clay modeling).
2. **Cognitive Development**: Observation, classification, spatial relationships, pattern recognition, and pre-number concepts.
3. **Language & Phonics**: Listening comprehension, phonemic awareness, vocabulary expansion, and storytelling.
4. **Socio-Emotional Development**: Sharing, self-expression, empathy, curiosity, and confidence.
