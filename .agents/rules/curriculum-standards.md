# ICSE Curriculum & Question Bank Formatting Rules

This rule defines how educational content, notes, and question papers must be formatted for this repository.

---

## 🚫 1. School-Agnostic & Redaction Rules (MANDATORY)

This repository serves independent homeschooling families and must remain completely **school-agnostic**.

### Prohibited Content
- **School Names**: Never include specific institution names, abbreviations, or acronyms (e.g., `The Frank Anthony Public School`, `FAPS`, `Bishop Cotton`, `Greenwood High`, `Bethany`, `National Public School`, `NPS`, `Delhi Public School`, `DPS`, etc.).
- **School Crests & Logos**: Never upload, link, or display images, headers, or watermarks containing school emblems or seals.
- **Examiner / Teacher Identifiers**: Strip out individual teacher names, signatures, student roll numbers, or specific class sections (e.g., "Class 7B", "Mrs. Sharma", "Exam Roll No: 12").
- **Scanned Textbook Pages**: Scanned textbook pages must not be hosted directly as images or raw PDFs. Extract the core textual concepts, outlines, and exercises into clean markdown instead.

### Standardized Replacements
When converting source tests and papers, substitute school-specific headers with standardized ICSE board terminology:

| Source Reference | Standardized Replacement |
| :--- | :--- |
| `The Frank Anthony Public School` / `FAPS` | `ICSE Model Assessment` / `ICSE Curriculum Examination` |
| `FAPS 7th Midterm Exam 2025` | `Class 7 Midterm Examination (Specimen Paper 2025)` |
| `FTE` (First Term Exam) | `First Term Examination (FTE) Model Paper` |
| `FUT` (First Unit Test) | `First Unit Test (FUT) Practice Paper` |
| `School QP` / `School solutions` | `Midterm Question Paper` / `Midterm Solutions Guide` |

## 2. CISCE Board Conventions
- **Language Standard**: British English spelling must be maintained across all content (e.g., *colour*, *aluminium*, *sulphate*, *programme*, *centre*, *behaviour*), in alignment with CISCE examination expectations.
- **Subject Grouping**: Maintain strict adherence to ICSE Group I (Compulsory: English, HCG, 2nd Lang), Group II (Math, Science, Comm), and Group III (Computer Applications, Commercial Apps).

---

## 3. Question Bank Structure & Mark Allocation
When creating question sets or chapter tests, follow this standard ICSE mark allocation:

### A. Objective & Multiple Choice Questions (1 Mark each)
- Direct recall or single-step computation.
- For MCQs, provide 4 options (A, B, C, D) with an explicit explanation of the correct answer.

### B. Short Answer / Give Reasons / Definitions (2 Marks each)
- Expected answer must contain **two distinct points or keywords**.
- Example for Physics/Chemistry:
  - *Question*: Why does water have a high specific heat capacity? (2 Marks)
  - *Answer*: Due to extensive hydrogen bonding between water molecules [1 Mark], requiring large thermal energy to raise its temperature [1 Mark].

### C. Short Structured Questions / Derivations (3 Marks each)
- 3 clear points, a 3-step derivation, or a neatly labeled diagram with 2 explanatory sentences.

### D. Long Structured Questions (4 – 5 Marks each)
- Formatted with sub-parts: (i), (ii), (iii) or (a), (b), (c).
- For numerical problems:
  - Formula stated: 1 Mark
  - Value substitution with converted SI units: 1 Mark
  - Correct final numerical answer with appropriate unit: 1 Mark

---

## 4. Worksheets & Practice Exercises
- Every worksheet must include:
  1. Estimated completion time (e.g., 30 minutes, 45 minutes).
  2. Total marks (e.g., 20 or 25 marks).
  3. Complete answer key / solution guide at the bottom (collapsible using `<details><summary>Click for Solutions</summary>...</details>`).
